A first round of Tottertop can end in a few seconds, before anyone has worked out what the tower wants. And when a round ends, the only question that matters is whether to play another. On November 15 we added three small things aimed at those two moments.

## Three tips that do not stop the game

In your very first round, three short tips appear one after another: watch the swinging block and tap; land it in the middle for a “Perfect!” and the tower wobbles less; if the tower leans, place the next block on the other side.

The tips never pause the game, and taps go straight through them to the tower. If the tower falls before the third tip, they start again in the next round. Players who already have a record do not see them, and “Show tips again” in Settings brings them back. A screen reader reads them aloud, and with Reduce Motion turned on they switch without animation.

## “New best!” when it happens

Before, you only learned you had beaten your best on the result screen, after the tower had already fallen. Now, the moment you go one floor past your best, a banner appears with a short vibration, and the best line at the top of the screen changes to “New best!”.

It compares against the best for the mode you are playing, free play or today's tower. It shows once per round, and not at all when you have no record yet.

## One number for the next try

The result card now has one extra line. If you finished below your best, it tells you how many floors you were short. If you matched or beat it, or this was your first round, it names the next title you have not earned yet and the height it needs. Once every title is earned, the line goes away. It works from what the game already stores, so nothing new is saved.

## What we have not checked

These changes are covered by automated tests and were looked at in simulator screenshots in English and Korean. The “floors to your best” line was only seen in tests, not on screen. None of it has been checked on a real phone yet. Nothing here is a release date.
