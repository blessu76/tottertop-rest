Tottertop is made for a short break: waiting for the bus, a few minutes before a meeting, the time it takes tea to cool. A round usually takes about a minute, and if the tower falls early it can be over in seconds.

## Why it is short

A long game asks for a long session before it is any fun. A tower gives a clear result after a few taps, and then the only question left is whether to play one more. The game is built around that question. When a round ends, the result screen shows how many floors you built in that round and your best record.

## What we cannot say yet

A minute is our own estimate from playing the game, not an average measured across many players. Until it is measured, read it as an estimate.
