Each round shows the next block before you drop the current one. The preview is the only look ahead the game gives you, and it is a small one: one block, not the whole tower.

## Using one step of warning

Once you know what is coming, you can decide where the current block should go. The first round's tip says it simply: when the tower leans, put the next block on the other side. That is advice for a leaning tower, not a rule. The physics still decides how the blocks settle.

## Color and the preview

The preview uses the color set you chose in Settings, the same as the blocks on the tower. Change the set and the preview changes with it.

## What the preview does not show

It shows the next block, not where that block will land. It is a picture of what comes next, not a forecast.

## What we have not checked

We have not tested whether the preview helps people score higher. That would take players, not a calculation.
