A game that asks for nothing is easier to explain than one that asks for something. Here is the rule we settled on and what it changes.

## The rule

Tottertop is meant to be completely free. There are no ads, no rewarded ads, no in-app purchases and no remove-ads option. We settled this on November 15, while the game is still in preparation, so nothing has to be taken back later.

## Unlocks come from playing

Block themes open through the titles you earn, and achievements are badges kept on your device. Nothing can be bought to get ahead, so nobody falls behind.

## Less to build

With no ads or purchases there is no ad code and no payment code to add or to keep up to date. The game itself needs no server of ours and no analytics SDK.

## What this does not say

It is not a release date. The game is still in preparation, and we will not name a day before there is one.
