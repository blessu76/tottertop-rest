This is a note about the journal itself, for anyone wondering what it is and what to expect from it.

## About once a day

A new post goes out at most once a day, at a time that changes from day to day. A slower rhythm is easier to keep honest, and it means each post can be about something that really happened in the last few days instead of something made up to fill a slot.

## Twelve languages, the same post

Every post is published in all twelve of the game's languages, in the same order as the language picker. We do not publish a post until every language is ready, so no one reads a half-translated page. Each translation gets a second review before it goes out.

## What goes in

Bugs we found and how we fixed them. Decisions, with the reason. Numbers from tests, with where they came from. And the things we have not checked, listed as not checked.

## What stays out

We will not promise a release date, and we will not announce features that do not exist yet. The game is in preparation and it is free; when something concrete happens — a build, a name change, a decision — it will be written here, and not before.

## If you want to follow along

The journal has a page for each language, and each post links to the others. There is nothing to sign up for.
