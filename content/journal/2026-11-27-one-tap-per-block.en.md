Tottertop is played with one tap for each block. A crane holds a block over the tower, and a tap lets it go. The tap decides when the block drops. The physics decides where it lands and how the tower moves afterwards.

## What the tap decides

Timing is the whole skill. A block dropped while it is centered over the tower lands cleanly, and the game shows "Perfect" when a block lands in the middle. A tap at any other moment still drops the block, but it lands off-center and the tower wobbles more.

## What the physics decides

It is a physics game, so blocks are not placed by hand. A block falls, touches the blocks below and settles, and the tower either steadies or leans on its own.

## What we have not checked

We have not checked how the tap feels on a real phone, with that phone's own touch delay. Until it is, count it as not checked.
