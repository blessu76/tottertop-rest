These are notes for the internal test (TestFlight) builds. The store release is not out yet.

## 1.0.0 (2) — 2026-11-11
- New look: night sky, stone blocks with soft contact shadows, a bigger result screen.
- Landing calls now come in three grades — Perfect / Great / OK — and blocks squash slightly when they land.
- Crumbling pieces and falling debris when the tower topples.
- Fixed: a drop could be called "Perfect" even when the block fell off right away.
- Fixed: the first block now sits on the base, and the tower's tilt is measured from it.
- Fixed: your best floor is saved the moment the tower falls, even if you leave during the slow-motion.

## 1.0.0 (1) — 2026-11-11
- First internal TestFlight build of Tottertop.
- Core loop: a crane swings, tap to drop, stack blocks; the tower tilts with its center of mass and topples if you miss.
- Five block shapes unlock as you climb; next-block preview; Daily seed (same order worldwide) and free play.
- Best floor saved on the device. 11 languages. No ads, no purchases in this build.

## 2026-11-09 — Renamed to Tottertop
- The game's name changed to Tottertop (기우뚱탑) after a store-name conflict. The site and everything on it now use the new name.

## 2026-11-08 — The intro site opened
- The Tottertop site opened at {{DOMAIN}} in 11 languages, with the game's home page, the contact page and the basics for search engines and AI agents (sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt).
