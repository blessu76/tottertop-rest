In short: the Site has no accounts, no sign-up, no comments and no advertising, and we do not ask you for personal information. The App is planned to show ads, as described in section 2.

## 1. The Site

- **Information you send us.** If you email us we receive your address and your message, and use them only to reply.
- **Hosting logs.** The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The host may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to deliver the pages. We do not receive or use it.
- **Browser storage.** The Site saves two small preferences, your light or dark theme and the language you pick, in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to us.
- **Links.** The Site links to ipe.rest and other sites. Their privacy practices are their own.

## 2. The App (in development)

The App is not released, so this section describes the plan, not a shipped product. Before release we will update it, and the privacy details in the app stores will match it.

- **Advertising.** The App is planned to be free to play and supported by ads from Google AdMob, mainly rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to continue a round). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob and its partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent first (for example, a consent message in the EEA and the UK, or the App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS). You can limit ad personalization or reset your advertising identifier in your device settings. Google describes its practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- **Remove-ads purchase.** The App is planned to offer an optional in-app purchase that removes ads. Payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we do not receive your card or payment details, only the purchase status needed to unlock the feature.
- **Game data.** Settings and progress such as your best score are planned to be stored on your device. We do not plan to require an account.
- **Analytics.** We have not decided whether the App will use analytics or crash reporting. If it does, this section will list what is collected before release.
